Features

Everything a spreadsheet can't do

You started networking in a Google Sheet. It worked until it didn't. Weaver picks up where spreadsheets break down.

Built for the coffee-chat grind.
From first cold email to closed referral — one place to keep every relationship warm.

Contact Database

Import your spreadsheet or add contacts one by one. Names, firms, positions, call history — all searchable, all sortable.

AI Call Notes

Upload a photo of your handwritten notes or type them in. AI summarizes key takeaways and logs them to the right contact.

Smart Reminders

Never ghost a contact. Set follow-up intervals for cold vs. warm contacts and get email reminders when it's time to reach out.

Gmail Sync

Connect Gmail, label threads "Weaver," and email timelines sync automatically. No manual logging.

Article Suggestions

Get 3 relevant articles per contact based on their industry, role, and your call notes — perfect for a reason to follow up.

Export to Excel

Your data is yours. Export everything to a clean spreadsheet anytime. No lock-in, no hostage situations.
