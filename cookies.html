Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Weaver also uses related browser storage technologies — localStorage and sessionStorage — which work the same way: they save small pieces of information in your browser so the app can function and remember you between visits. In this policy, "cookies" refers to all of these technologies.

2. How we use them

Weaver uses cookies and browser storage for two purposes:

  • Strictly necessary — to keep you logged in, maintain your session, store your contacts and settings locally so the app works, and remember your cookie choice. The app cannot function without these, so they are always on and do not require consent.
  • Non-essential — anything beyond the above, such as product analytics or usage measurement that helps us understand how Weaver is used. These are only set if you agree.

4. Your choices

When you first visit Weaver, we ask whether you accept non-essential cookies. You can accept, or reject them and continue using Weaver with only the strictly necessary cookies. You can change your decision at any time:

You can also clear or block cookies through your browser settings. Note that blocking strictly necessary cookies/storage will prevent Weaver from working, including keeping you logged in.

Gmail and third parties When you connect Gmail, Google may set its own cookies during the sign-in flow, governed by Google's own policies. Payments are handled by Stripe, which may set cookies needed to process a transaction securely. These are controlled by those providers, not by Weaver. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle data from these services.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy as the product evolves. We will post the updated version here and update the "Last updated" date. If we introduce new non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent again.
