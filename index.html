Playbook

The principles behind Weaver. The detailed method is built into every draft, prep sheet, plan and reminder Weaver makes for you, so you can focus on the people.

  • Be curious about them first. People remember how a conversation felt more than what you asked.
  • Write down specifics so you can bring them up later. It shows you were listening.
  • Share your own story honestly, not a rehearsed pitch.
  • Have a real reason for choosing each person, and say it.
  • Keep it short and easy to say yes to.
  • Timing and presentation matter. Weaver builds this into every draft for you.
  • Prepare, then listen. Your best questions will come from what they say.
  • Make sure they learn who you are before the time runs out.
  • End with a clear next step.
  • Thank them, specifically.
  • Act on their advice, then tell them you did.
  • Stay in touch with the people who matter most. Weaver reminds you when.
  • Earn it through the relationship before you ask.
  • Ask clearly, and make it easy for them to help.
  • Close the loop: tell them how it turned out.
  • Consistency beats bursts. A steady weekly rhythm wins a season.
  • Every good conversation should lead to the next one.
  • Go wide: more than one school network, more than one city.
