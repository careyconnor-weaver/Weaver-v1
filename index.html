Say theright thing.

Drafts written from your own notes, in your voice, matched to where the relationship stands. You edit every word before it goes out.

Draft · to Sophie Laurent (example)

Thank you, and a quick follow-up

Hi Sophie,

Thank you again for Tuesday. Your advice to practice a paper LBO before the superday changed how I’m preparing.

If the offer still stands, I’d love to take you up on the mock interview in November.

Make it sound like you before you send it.Edit

Walk inprepared.

A scout report on their firm and group before every call: recent news with sources, common ground, and questions a search engine can’t answer.

  • Restructuring team expanded in New York2

Ask

  • What surprised you most moving from M&A to restructuring?

Upload a spreadsheet

Upload your networking tracker spreadsheet (CSV or Excel format)

Required columns

  • Name - Contact's full name
  • Company - Company name (synonyms: Firm, Bank, Organization, Org)
  • Contact Date - Contact date(s) in MM/DD/YEAR format
  • Date format: Use MM/DD/YEAR (e.g., 1/15/2024). Multiple dates can be separated by commas, colons, or semicolons.

Before you upload, check that:

  • Contacts are in the FIRST tab
  • Headers start in cell A1

Optional columns

  • Position, Title, Role
  • Phone, Email, Location
  • Priority (Low, Medium, High)
  • Any other columns saved as notes

Headers must start in cell A1 of the first tab.

Follow-up guidance

For cold emails: Follow up 1-2 times with 10+ days between each.

For established contacts: Check in once a quarter (~90 days).

The best way to strengthen relationships is to do them a favor and expect nothing in return. Send them an article relevant to their job or interests a couple times a year.
